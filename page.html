Method

Three steps, and only the first one is yours.

Bookkeeping fails at the counter, not at the desk — the entry never gets made because making it interrupts the sale. So the only thing BolKhata asks of you is the sentence you were going to say anyway.

  1. 01Speak

    Say what happened

    One sentence, in the words you'd use with the customer standing in front of you. No form to open, no field to find, no app to learn.

  2. 02Resolve

    The agent works it out

    It identifies the party, the amount, the direction the money moved, and the account it belongs in. When something is genuinely unclear it asks one short question — never a form.

  3. 03Post

    The books close themselves

    Entries post to a double-entry ledger, balances update, and receivables start their clock. Your day is reconciled before you lock up.

Oversight

An agent you can audit.

An agent writing to your books is only acceptable if you can see what it did, disagree with it, and take the whole record elsewhere. All three are built in, not promised.

  • Every entry shows its source

    Open any posting and read the sentence that created it, word for word, beside what it produced.

  • Correct it by speaking

    “That was Priya, not Ramesh.” The agent re-posts and leaves the correction in the trail rather than overwriting it.

  • Nothing large posts silently

    Set an amount above which entries wait for your confirmation. The agent holds them and tells you why.

  • The books are yours

    Export the full ledger whenever you like, in formats that will outlive any single app — including this one.
